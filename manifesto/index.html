Values

Three things we will not compromise on. For any client. At any price.

01

Prompting is the craft.

Any studio can call itself AI-native. What separates a great website from AI slop is the quality of the prompts, the evaluations, and the human review behind every output. That is where we invest.

02

Craft does not expire.

Every codebase we ship, we would be comfortable with another engineer opening six months later. Clean architecture, semantic markup, and zero hallucinated dependencies. Not optional at any timeline.

03

Northbound is woman-owned and operated. We believe the industry changes when women do not just have a seat at the table. They own the studio.
