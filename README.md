# Northbound Studio

**Northbound** is an AI-native product studio built to ship web, mobile, and AI-enabled products with a fixed scope, fixed price, and fixed timeline.

## What this folder contains

- `northbound.html` — the landing page mockup for the Northbound studio.
- `business-plan.md` — the strategic business plan, market positioning, and go-to-market framework.
- `todo.md` — the early launch task list with prioritized workstreams.
- `launch-checklist.md` — high-impact launch and delivery actions for the first 90 days.
- `deploy.md` — recommended steps for publishing `northboundsoftwarestudio.com`.
- `pitch-deck-outline.md` — slide-by-slide deck structure for pitching Northbound.
- `sales-sequence.md` — founder outreach sequence and call agenda.
- `offer-sheet.md` — one-page description of packages, pricing, and delivery promise.

## Why Northbound exists

Traditional agencies and boutique studios still sell time, uncertainty, and vague deliverables. Northbound competes by packaging outcomes into clear, purchase-ready products backed by AI-native execution.

The core promise is: product shipped on a known date, at a known price, with senior engineering oversight and AI agents optimizing delivery.

## Where to start

1. Open `northbound.html` in a browser and review the messaging, package structure, and CTA flow.
2. Read `business-plan.md` to align on strategy, audience, pricing, and positioning.
3. Follow `todo.md` to turn the concept into a first launch-ready business.
4. Use `launch-checklist.md` as the tactical guide to launch the service and start selling.
