The projects I've been working on as of late

Five products, built end to end by one person — the database, the API, the interface, the tests and the deployment. Four of them price, score or judge something a business acts on, which is why three of the five draw an explicit line between what an AI model is allowed to decide and what plain code must compute. ZipQuarry

Live · app.zipquarry.com

A metal detector for salespeople. Draw a circle on a map; it finds every local business inside it that looks like it needs what you sell, scores them, and writes each one a first email that mentions something real about them.

Quotefront

Live · no sign-in needed

A customer photographs a job on their phone and has a real price in about a minute, worked out from that contractor's own rates with every line of the arithmetic shown. The easiest of the five to try — the customer half needs no account at all.

TypeScriptNext.js 16 · React 19Drizzle · Neon Claude visionMulti-tenantTurborepo
Getting in
No account: /q/apex-roofing and /q/cedar-line-fencing. The contractor dashboard is a passwordless link, invite-only.
Notable
The model never prices anything. It describes the photos; a pure, tested function does every sum.

ReconAI

Runs locally

A supplier sends a bill. ReconAI checks it against what was actually ordered — every line, every sum — and returns approve, hold or reject, with each disagreement named and priced. The job an accounts-payable clerk does by hand.

TypeScriptNext.js 16 · React 19Claude Tool-forced JSONStatelessNo database
Getting in
No sign-in exists — no account, no database, nothing stored. Clone, add an API key, npm run dev. Or press Preview sample output and the real engine runs with no key at all.
Notable
376 lines decide whether a company pays a bill, and they contain no AI call and no network.
ReconAI at a glance →

Shipshape

Runs locally · brand page live

Most tools track what you are doing. Shipshape also tracks what "done" is supposed to mean — written once, applied to every project you own — and turns whatever falls short into cards on that project's board.

TypeScriptNext.jsDrizzle · Neon 14 tables49 tests passingpnpm · Turborepo
Getting in
Brand page is public: shipshape-brand. The app runs locally; sign-in is a passwordless link printed to the terminal.
Notable
One nullable foreign key — a card knowing which criterion it satisfies — is what stops the standard decaying into a document nobody opens.
