Deep Dive Agentic AI & the 30-day build Jun 2026

Why agentic AI changes what's possible in a fixed-scope sprint

Traditional shops quote 90 days because they're padding for unknown unknowns. Agentic systems — models that loop through plan, act, and verify steps autonomously — compress the routine work that accounts for most of that time. What used to take a sprint now takes a session.

The more interesting shift isn't speed. It's scope confidence. Agents surface edge cases before a line of implementation code exists, run test suites in real time, and maintain consistent style across a large codebase without drift. That's what makes fixed-scope, fixed-price delivery viable at a quality bar that used to require a large team.

Common questions

Answers to the things founders ask before they book a call.

How long does a build actually take?
Northbound delivers in 3–30 days depending on the package. Spark (template landing page) ships in 3 days, Launch (custom marketing site) in 7 days, Augment (AI feature added to your product) in 14 days, and Build (full web or mobile app) in 30 days. Pulse is an ongoing monthly subscription with no fixed end date. Build timelines are committed dates, not estimates — if we miss, you get a discount.
What is the Discovery Sprint and do I need it?
The Discovery Sprint ($1,000 CAD) is an add-on to any of our packages — Spark, Launch, Build, or Augment. When you add it to your project, we layer in CEO-level business evaluation alongside the build: reviewing your competitive landscape, user journey, and product direction to design the features that will actually move the needle. It adds 10 build days to your engagement and includes three dedicated calls (kick-off, mid-sprint, delivery) so there's real strategic alignment throughout. You also get a written product brief and roadmap to keep. If you're not sure what to build next, or want outside perspective before we start coding, this is the add-on for that.
What does fixed-price actually mean?
It means the number you see is the number you pay — no hourly billing, no scope-creep invoices, no surprise charges. Launch includes three rounds of corrections after delivery. If we discover something harder than expected mid-build, that's our problem to solve. If you want to add something new, it becomes a new waybill at a new fixed price.
Can you add AI features to something we already have?
Yes — that's the Augment package ($6,000 CAD, 14 days). We integrate chat, search, summarization, or agent workflows into your existing codebase, design the prompts, set up evals so you can measure quality, and walk your team through everything on handoff.
What is AI-native development and why does it matter?
AI-native development uses agentic systems — models that plan, act, and verify autonomously — to compress the repetitive 70% of any build. Agents surface edge cases before a line of code is written, run tests in real time, and hold style consistent across a large codebase. A senior engineer designs and reviews everything before it ships. The result: faster builds, lower cost, and fewer post-launch surprises than a traditional shop.
What do I get at the end?
A deployed, production-ready product — not a staging link. Plus the full source code (you own it, forever), and a handoff doc a non-developer can actually read. There are no retainer strings attached.
What is Pulse and how does social media management work?
Pulse is a $750 CAD/month subscription for social media management — no fixed contract, cancel anytime. We handle 12–15 posts per month across your channels: content writing, branded graphics, scheduling, and posting. It's built for restaurant owners, boutiques, coaches, consultants, and any local business that wants a consistent presence without hiring someone full-time. Book a scope call and we'll align on your channels, voice, and content mix before the first post goes out.
Why don't you have a chatbot on your site?
Because we think it would make your experience worse, not better. A chatbot on a contact page is one of the most common ways companies apply AI without thinking through whether it actually helps — and in most cases it adds friction, gives generic answers, and makes people feel like they're being fobbed off. We believe in using AI where it genuinely improves an outcome: faster builds, better code quality, tighter scope. For a conversation about your project, a real email or a 20-minute call beats any widget. That's why the CTA on this page is a call, not a chat window.
Where are you based, and do you work with teams outside Toronto?
We're based in Toronto (YYZ) and work with founders and product teams worldwide. Remote-first from day zero — the scoping call, weekly demos, and handoff are all async-friendly.
