Case studies

How we build, shown on our own products

Each one starts with a real problem, shows how we solved it, and gives three numbers you can check in the code.

01
Joseki
Online game · built in 3 days
Open it ↗

A game server has to be right about every single move

Joseki is a place to play the board game Go online. One rules engine on the server checks every move, so no game can ever reach an illegal position. The computer opponents run a real AI model right in your browser, and a test fails if what the website says about that AI stops matching what it actually does.

  • 3days from the first line of code to joseki.online
  • 2,700+automated tests that run on every change
  • 31→92%how sure the AI gets about the same move, from beginner to expert level

What it means for you The rules of your business live in one place, and tests catch mistakes before your customers do.

02
ZipQuarry
Sales tool · for small businesses
Open it ↗

Owners lose their evenings looking for new customers

ZipQuarry finds businesses within a set distance of any ZIP or postal code. It reads each business's own website for a contact email, then drafts a first message for a person to review. Every address is saved with the page it came from and the date, so you can always check where it came from.

  • 3.6sseconds to find 200 local businesses, on the first real run
  • 58%of businesses found have a public email (1,021 of 1,770)
  • 67groups of automated tests

What it means for you Honest numbers, even when they aren't flattering. When 42% of businesses have no public email, the app says so instead of guessing.

03
Quotefront
Quoting app · for contractors
Open it ↗

Contractors write quotes at night from job photos

A customer sends photos and measurements. AI looks at the photos and describes what it sees. Then plain code, not the AI, turns that into a price range using the contractor's own rates, with line items that add up exactly. If the photo check fails, the request is still saved and flagged, so no customer is lost.

  • 0prices made up by the AI. Code does all the math.
  • 6trades supported, from roofing to paving
  • 3.2×the most the top of a price range can be over the bottom, enforced by a test

What it means for you AI where it helps, plain code where it has to be exact. That is how we add AI to your product.

PHASE 1 / MEASURE DAYS 1–10

We time it first

We sit with the people doing the work and time each task: how long it takes, how often, and who does it. You get a clear picture of where the hours go, and what fixing it is worth in dollars.

PHASE 2 / BUILD DAYS 11–45

Built into the tools you already use

We automate the two or three tasks that save the most time. They connect to your email, calendar, forms, accounting and customer lists. AI writes drafts where it helps. A person approves anything that goes to a customer.

PHASE 3 / HAND OVER BY DAY 60

Measured again, then it's yours

We time the same tasks again, train your team, and give you the code, the logins and a how-to guide. The result fits on one page: before, after, and how much time you saved.

Your way out on day 10

If the numbers show it isn't worth it, we tell you on day 10 and stop. You pay for those ten days only, and you keep the notes and measurements. We would rather lose the job than sell you something that won't pay off.

Common questions

Answers to what people ask before they email us.

Show questions about
I have an app idea but no tech team. Can you help?
Yes. This is the most common reason people email us. You tell us what the app should do, in your own words. In the first two days we turn that into a written plan with a fixed price and a launch date. You don't need to know how to code, and you own everything we build.
Do you work with restaurants?
Yes. We build fast restaurant websites in 3 days, automate booking reminders and review replies, and run your social media with Pulse. We start by timing where your hours go, so we only build what will really save you time.
How long does it take?
It depends on the package. A website (Spark) takes 3 days. Fixing software you inherited (Salvage) takes 5 days. A custom website (Launch) takes 7 days. Adding AI to your product (Augment) takes 14 days. A full app (Build) takes 30 days. Automate is done by day 60, with a go or stop decision on day 10. Moorage and Pulse are monthly plans with no end date. These are promised dates, not guesses. If we are late, you pay less.
What is the Discovery Sprint? Do I need it?
It's an add-on for any package. We look at your business the way an experienced product leader would: your competitors, your customers and where your product should go next. It adds 10 days of building, three calls (start, middle and end), and a written plan you keep. If you're not sure what to build next, or you want an outside opinion before we start, this is for you.
What does fixed price mean?
We agree on one price before we build anything, and that's what you pay. No hourly billing and no surprise charges. Launch includes three rounds of changes after delivery. If something turns out harder than we expected, that's our problem, not yours. If you want to add something new, we give you a new fixed price for it.
Can you add AI to a product we already have?
Yes, that's the Augment package (14 days). Adding AI is the easy part. The hard part is what goes wrong later: the AI makes things up, the bill grows too fast, nobody can tell if a change helped, and your team can't maintain it. We fix all four. We check every AI answer, set spending limits for each customer, build tests that score quality, and teach your team how it all works.
How do you use AI to build faster?
AI tools handle the repetitive parts of building software, which is most of the work. They can plan a task, do it and check it. They also spot problems early and run tests as we go. A senior developer designs the product and checks everything before it ships. The result is faster builds, lower cost and fewer surprises after launch.
What do I get at the end?
A working product that real customers can use, not a test link. You also get all of the code, which you own forever, and a simple guide that someone who doesn't code can read. No ongoing contract required.
What is Pulse?
Pulse is a monthly social media plan with no long contract. Cancel anytime. We write and design 12 to 15 posts a month for your channels, then schedule and post them. It's made for restaurants, shops, coaches, consultants and any local business that wants to stay active online without hiring someone. Email us and we'll agree on your channels and style before the first post.
Why is there no chatbot on your site?
Because we think it would make your visit worse. Chatbots on websites often give generic answers and make people feel brushed off. We use AI where it really helps: building faster, writing better code and making clearer plans. To talk about your project, a real email from a real person works better. That's why this page asks you to email us.
Where are you based? Do you work outside Toronto?
We're based in Toronto and work with clients anywhere. Everything can be done remotely: planning, weekly demos and handover.
What can Automate actually do?
It takes over the repetitive work between your apps: new requests, quotes, reminders, chasing documents, data entry, reports and first drafts of routine emails. We start by timing how you do it today, so we pick what to automate based on real numbers. When a task needs judgment, the automation gets it ready and a person decides.
What if automation isn't worth it for us?
Then we tell you on day 10. You only pay for the first 10 days, and you keep the notes and measurements. We only build when the numbers show it will pay for itself.
We have software nobody understands. Can you help?
Yes, that's Salvage (5 days). You get a written map of how your software works, a safe way to make changes, a check of security risks, and our advice on whether to fix it or replace it, with a price for each option. You don't have to hire us for the next step.
What is Moorage? Do I have to buy it?
No. Moorage is an optional monthly care plan for software we built for you. We keep it updated and secure, check that it stays up and fast, test your backups, and make small changes each month. You own the code either way, and you can cancel any month.
