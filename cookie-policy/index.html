Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Northbound Software Studio ("Northbound", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help the site remember your preferences, improve performance, and provide analytics about how the site is used.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to operate (security, session management). These cannot be disabled without affecting basic functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — used to collect aggregated, non-identifying information about site usage (page views, time on page). We use privacy-oriented analytics that do not track you across other sites.
  • Third-party cookies — cookies set by external services we embed or link to (for example, booking or embedded content). These are controlled by those third parties; review their policies for details.

How to control cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not prevent you from using the site, but may reduce our ability to improve it. For instructions, see the help pages for your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

More information

For details about how we handle personal data generally, see our Privacy Policy. If you have questions about cookies or data practices, contact us at hello@northboundsoftwarestudio.com.
