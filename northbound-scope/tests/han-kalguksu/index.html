Han Kalguksu has been feeding North York from this spot on Yonge Street with a single commitment: hand-cut noodles made fresh every morning, served in a broth that takes its time.

Our king-size dumplings are meant to be shared. The pork cutlet is the real thing. Come in when you're cold — come back when you're not. The regulars know.
