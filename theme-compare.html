Theme Guide

01 · Current

Blueprint

Clean Swiss grid, paper/ink/cobalt. The current site. Strong, technical, precise. Safe bet — nothing to fix.

02 · Same palette + texture

Grain

Dopple-inspired risograph grain overlay on a warmer cream base. Feels handmade and craft-forward despite being a software studio — the contrast is the point.

03 · Inverted

Midnight

Deep navy dark mode. Cobalt and yellow pop harder. More atmospheric, confident. Can feel cold to some clients — test with your target niche.

04 · Dopple-adjacent

Warm Press

Parchment paper, rust accent (vs cobalt), grain on everything. Print-house editorial energy. Most differentiated vs other dev studios. Risk: less "technical" signal.
