# Pulse — Social Subscription Onboarding

**Manifest line:** Social media management — 12–15 posts/month across the client's channels · $750 CAD / month · cancel anytime

Pulse is the only recurring waybill: ongoing social content handled end to end — written,
designed, scheduled, posted, and summarized monthly. There's no build and no ship date; there's
a monthly rhythm. Your job on this call is to capture the brand voice, the channels, the content
mix, account access, and — critically — the approval workflow and the cadence of raw material,
because that's what keeps the calendar full.

## When this is the right waybill

✅ A restaurant owner who needs weekly Instagram and Facebook posts.
✅ A boutique that wants to stay active on TikTok without hiring full-time.
✅ A coach or consultant building a consistent LinkedIn presence.
✅ Any local business that wants social handled so they can focus on serving customers.

🚦 **Redirect / pair if you hear:** "I need a website too" → `scope-spark` or `scope-launch`
alongside Pulse. "Run our paid ads" / "produce a video shoot" / "manage all our DMs" → out of
Pulse's base scope; flag as separate work (see boundary).

## How to run this call

Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` doctrine: one question at a time, never assume, draw the line. Pulse
clients are usually non-technical and busy — keep it friendly and quick, and be explicit that
the monthly rhythm depends on them feeding you material and approving on time.

## The onboarding checklist

### 1 — The business and the goal
- What's the business, and what should social actually achieve — foot traffic, bookings,
  awareness, leads, community?
- What does a good month look like to them?

### 2 — Channels 🚩
- 🚩 Which platforms — Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Google Business? Primary vs.
  secondary, and how the 12–15 monthly posts split across them.
- 🚩 Existing handles, follower base, and **account access** — who owns the accounts, and can we
  get the admin / Meta Business / scheduler access we need to post?

### 3 — Brand voice and content 🚩
- 🚩 Voice and tone — playful, premium, expert, neighborly? Words and topics to use and avoid.
- Content pillars / mix — promotions, behind-the-scenes, educational, events, menu/product
  features, user-generated content?
- 🚩 Visual identity — logo, colors, fonts, existing photography. **Where do photos come from
  going forward** — the client sends them, stock, existing library? (Pulse provides *branded
  graphics*, not photography/video production — set that expectation.)

### 4 — Source material and cadence 🚩
- 🚩 Who feeds the raw info — promos, events, menu changes — and **how and when** (a monthly
  intake, a shared folder, a quick call)?
- Posting frequency and best times; any seasonal or event-driven spikes; blackout dates.
- Hashtag / geo strategy. Community management — do we reply to comments and DMs? (Usually **out
  of base scope** unless agreed — flag it.)

### 5 — Approval workflow 🚩
- 🚩 Approve a **monthly batch in advance**, or per post? What's the client's turnaround
  commitment, and who is the approver? (Late approvals = a thin month — say so.)

### 6 — Compliance and sensitivities 🚩
- Regulated claims (health, finance, alcohol), promo/contest rules, anything off-limits.

### 7 — Reporting 🚩
- 🚩 Which metrics matter — reach, engagement, follows, clicks, bookings? Confirm we have the
  native analytics access needed for the monthly summary.

### 8 — Onboarding logistics 🚩
- 🚩 Account access handoff, brand assets, the first month's content calendar to approve, and the
  start date.

## Scope boundary (subscription discipline)

**In scope (each month):** 12–15 posts across the agreed channels · copywriting · branded
graphics · scheduling and posting · a monthly performance summary. Cancel anytime.

**Out of base scope (flag as separate / future work):** paid ad management and ad spend ·
photography or video production · influencer outreach · full community management (replying to
all comments/DMs) unless agreed · websites or landing pages (→ Spark / Launch) · large one-off
design projects.

**The dependency, stated plainly:** the monthly rhythm depends on timely raw material and
approvals from the client; without them, the calendar slips.

## What we must have before the first post

- [ ] Account access for every channel we'll post to
- [ ] Brand assets and the voice/tone guidance
- [ ] Content pillars / mix agreed
- [ ] The cadence and method for receiving raw material
- [ ] The approval workflow and the named approver
- [ ] The reporting metrics that matter
- [ ] The start date
